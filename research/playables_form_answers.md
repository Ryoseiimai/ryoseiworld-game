## Describe your game(s)
RYOSEIWORLD is a short, story-driven pixel-art RPG for mobile and desktop browsers (about 20 minutes, 4 chapters + epilogue, Japanese text).
A 12-year-old boy who only plays games meets an AI on his phone that can "make anything you say."
Each boss is something that blocks him: BUG KING (self-doubt), a living-room wall that speaks in his parents' voice, the King of Comparison who throws other people's numbers, and finally a grey copy of himself who can't be defeated by attacking — only by choosing "make" three times.
Turn-based battles with a rolling HP meter (you can survive if you heal before it reaches zero) and new commands unlocked each chapter (items, inspect, call allies).
Single HTML file, no external requests, all art is original. Integrated with the Playables SDK (save/load, pause/resume, audio) and responsive for any aspect ratio.
